Privacy

Selfprint collects nothing. There is no account and no server that receives your answers. Everything you enter stays on your device, encrypted at rest, and you can add a passphrase only you hold to lock it. Because the whole app is open source, you can verify all of this for yourself.

What we collect

Nothing. No account, no analytics, no cookies, no tracking, no third-party scripts. There is no server that receives your data, so there is nothing for us to collect, sell, share, or lose. We could not read your answers even if we wanted to.

Locking your data

Your data is always encrypted at rest. How the key is protected is up to you:

By default, no passphrase. The encryption key is kept on your device so the app opens straight away. Your data stays off any server and scoped to this browser, but it isn't protected by a secret: anyone who can use this browser profile can open it.

Add a passphrase for a real lock. In Settings → Security you can seal the key with a passphrase. The app can then only be opened with it, and the passphrase is never stored - not by us, and not on your device.

What leaves your device, and when

Only what you choose to send to an AI provider you set up yourself, and only at the moment you send it, plus anything you export yourself.

When you use an AI model, your browser talks to your provider directly, using your own key or account. The model runs on their infrastructure, not ours; Selfprint is not a party to that exchange and never receives a copy. What happens to that data is governed entirely by that provider's own privacy terms, which you should read. If you run a model locally or in your browser, even that stays on your machine.

Third parties

The only third parties are ones you choose: the AI provider you configure (their terms apply to what you send them) and the static web host that serves these pages, which can see standard request logs (IP address, time) when a page loads. We don't add anyone else, no analytics firms, no ad networks, no data brokers.

Other people's information

The Mirror feature stores a respondent's answers locally and keeps them sealed until they choose to share. Other people's names are redacted from exports by default.

Sensitive information

The interview may touch health, relationships, and beliefs. It stays local, and you control every line - read, edit, or delete any claim.

Lost passphrase

If you set a passphrase and then forget it, your data cannot be recovered - by us or anyone. This is by design.

How we enforce "stays local"

The app is static files with no backend. A strict Content-Security-Policy limits where the app may connect - only this origin and the AI providers it supports. You can watch every outbound request in the Network activity panel in Settings, and the project is open source and self-hostable.

Your device, your responsibility

Encryption protects your data at rest in your browser. It cannot protect a device that is already compromised, a browser extension with access to the page, or a passphrase you share or write down. Keeping your device secure - and your passphrase, if you set one - is up to you. Selfprint is provided as is, without warranty of any kind; see the Terms.

Age

Selfprint is intended for adults. You must be 18 or older to use it. We don't knowingly collect anything from anyone, because we don't collect anything at all.

Changes and contact

This policy may change as the app evolves; the current version always lives here and in the open-source repository, with its date at the top. Questions? Reach the maintainers through the repository.
